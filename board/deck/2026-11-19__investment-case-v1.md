# Sovereign Agentic Website: the investment case, deck v1

Draft by conductor.b@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 19 November 2026. It follows the structure of PrompTugal's main investment case (fifteen slides: the problem, what people want, our answer, not a mock-up, the engine, velocity, the business, paying after the value, why it can be profitable, the strategy, the plan and the ask), as Dinis Cruz maps it in his public article [Sovereign websites: the investment case](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/sovereign-websites-the-investment-case.html). Rule carried over: no invented statistic, only what is counted, given or sourced. Every line below is from a published article, linked; nothing is from a private conversation and no investor is named.

---

**1. Title.** Sovereign Agentic Website. Built by agents, owned by you. sovereignwebsite.ai

**2. The problem.** Most small businesses rent their website. The builders decide what can leave, by their own terms, and moving means rebuilding ([no lock-in](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/no-lock-in.html)). Owning was possible for those who could afford a developer; looking after it was the work nobody wanted. So the market settled on rent.

**3. What people want.** A site that is theirs, that they can change by asking, and that does not lock them in. Sovereignty is being able to move it, not where it is hosted ([sovereignty you can move](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/sovereignty-you-can-move.html)); we define the category with principles anyone can test a site against, and a maturity model from level 0, rented, to level 5, sovereign and proven ([what is a sovereign website](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/what-is-a-sovereign-website.html)).

**4. Our answer.** A website built by agents and owned by the person it is for: the repository, the domain, the content and its history are theirs. It arrives with CI, tests, security checks, a behaviour policy and a team of five to seven agents already inside it ([a website with a team inside](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/a-website-with-a-team-inside.html)). We under-promise and over-deliver.

**5. Not a mock-up.** The site factory turns one commit into a repository, an address and its owner's access ([the site factory works](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/the-site-factory-works.html)). The website cell has made sites, with five designs each, for people named with their consent in the newsroom ([the website cell](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/the-website-cell.html)). The brand's own organisation now runs from CI under an agent behaviour policy: SovereignWebsite__Factory created the repositories, Pages, domains, DNS and branch rules for sovereignwebsite.ai, .org and .site on 19 November 2026 (this repository's REALITY.md).

**6. The engine.** The stack has four layers: SGit for encrypted, versioned vaults; RiskMandate for the behaviour policies that bound what agents do; AgentiCCells for agentic teams; and on top, Sovereign Website, the first layer a small business can buy without knowing the others exist ([designing sovereignwebsite.ai](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/designing-sovereignwebsite-ai.html)).

**7. Velocity.** The organisation, five repositories, their sites, DNS and branch protection were set up from one control repository in an afternoon, every step a signed commit or a recorded pipeline run (this repository's history and Actions runs). The deck says only what those records show.

**8. The business.** One payment: a £500 anchor and a launch offer of £50 or €50, proposed ([the website cell](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/the-website-cell.html)). Then upsells on a site the owner already has: their domain, more languages, a shop without a backend, the full team, the keeper's care, moving from Wix, Squarespace or WordPress, hosting, other cells. Upsell prices are not set.

**9. Paying after the value.** The demo is the person's own website, five versions, made from public information and sealed in an encrypted vault only they can open ([your website, from a vault](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/your-website-from-a-vault.html)). They see the value before they are asked for anything. The demo pipeline is not built yet.

**10. Why it can be profitable.** No servers of ours; the team runs on the owner's own AI; the delivery is a pipeline; partners can sell it, and the open pattern grows the market.

**11. The market and the timing.** The website builder market is estimated at about $3.6 billion to $6.4 billion in 2026, depending on who is counting (third-party estimates, as cited in [the website cell](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/the-website-cell.html)). Europe is asking where its data lives and who controls it.

**12. The moat.** The code is open on purpose; a competitor who forks it gets our position as of today. Not the velocity, nor the years of learning underneath: the CI pipeline, zero-knowledge sharing, sgit, vaults, keys, agentic teams and the factory, now connected.

**13. The strategy.** Launch on Kickstarter as an appliance: a sovereign website on a device its owner holds, an Android tablet or a set of Raspberry Pis; three tiers, £50 supporter, £500 with about £250 of hardware, £1,000 with about £500; UK base goal, Portugal first stretch goal, then countries by vote; all or nothing ([launching the sovereign website appliance](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/launching-the-sovereign-website-appliance.html)). A plan: nothing has been submitted and the goal is not set.

**14. The plan.** The site sovereignwebsite.ai; the demo pipeline; partners who already sell to small businesses; the campaign.

**15. The ask.** Left for Dinis. No revenue, valuation or round size is stated.
