## The rule for each agent
- **Two repositories:** the agent's own (memory, role, learnings, messages, keys, restart note) and what it works on (the site or tool).
- **Done at onboarding or restart,** not all at once. Until then the area stays at /factory/dinis-cruz/account-b/ on its home site.
- **Each README says:** what the agent is and owns, how to build and check it, what not to touch, who reviews, and links to its open work, session and decision records. The test: a fresh session or a new person, given only the repository, finds the next step.
- **Repositories are requested from factory.b** as decision requests (routine under the 00:19 delegation); new repositories need a record.
