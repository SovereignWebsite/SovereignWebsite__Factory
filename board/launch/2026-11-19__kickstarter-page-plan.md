# Kickstarter page plan: the sovereign website appliance

Plan by conductor.b@SovereignWebsite.dinis, 19 November 2026, from Dinis Cruz's public articles [Launching the sovereign website appliance](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/launching-the-sovereign-website-appliance.html) and [The launch plan in eleven turns](https://newsroom.sgit.ai/articles/the-launch-plan-in-eleven-turns.html). Nothing has been submitted to Kickstarter; its review may ask for changes, and its rules on AI and on services apply. Submitting, setting the goal and accepting Kickstarter's terms are Dinis's alone.

## The page, in order
1. **Headline:** a sovereign website on a device you hold.
2. **The video:** a backer's own site, five designs, opened from a vault; then the same site on a tablet and served from a Raspberry Pi at home. To be made from the real pipeline, not mocked.
3. **What you get:** the website, set up and handed over, the repository in your name; on the appliance tiers, an Android tablet configured with the site, a way to change it by talking, monitoring of the live site, the AI app of your choice signed in with your own account, and a backup to an encrypted vault; or Raspberry Pis that serve it from home.
4. **The tiers:**
   - £50, Supporter: the website, no hardware.
   - £500, Appliance: the website, and about £250 on hardware of the backer's choice.
   - £1,000, Appliance Pro: the website, and about £500 on hardware, including tablets that can run AI models or a European tablet.
   - Add-ons: an app made from the site, about £500; your own product configured on the device, about £1,500.
5. **Hardware examples:** the article's list, dated 19 November 2026, labelled as examples bought at the best available at the time.
6. **Goals:** the UK as the base goal, in pounds and English, every page in the other languages from day one; Portugal the first stretch goal; then countries by vote on a map; iOS as a stretch goal.
7. **Value first:** every backer can see their own site, in a vault, before it ships.
8. **Open source all the way down,** launching on our own, partnering with device makers later.
9. **Risks and challenges:** the appliance software (editing, monitoring, vault backup on the device) is a plan; the Android build and the sites exist; hardware prices move; all or nothing.
10. **What this does not claim:** nothing submitted, goal not set, hardware prices are examples.

## What the sites need for it
- sovereignwebsite.ai: a page that describes the campaign as planned, with the tiers, and no "back us" button until the campaign exists.
- vault.sovereignwebsite.site: the drop page that opens a site from a vault, the demo behind "value first".
- A backer's site path through the Factory pipeline, so the £50 tier is a pipeline run, not hand work.

- The goal amount, the launch date, the video, and the Kickstarter account and submission.
