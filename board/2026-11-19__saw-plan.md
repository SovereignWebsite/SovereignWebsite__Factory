## 1. Strategy map (one page)

**One line.** A website built by agents from what you tell them, and owned by the person it is for. Built by agents, owned by you.

**What sovereignty means (our definition).** Sovereignty is not where a site is hosted; it is whether you can move it. Four tests (sovereignty-you-can-move):
1. Can you move it? Static files in your own repository, with history, on any host.
2. Do you own the schema? Structure and content are files in open formats in your repository.
3. Who sets the terms? You: no platform, no subscription, open code nobody can withdraw.
4. What if we are bought? Nothing changes: you already hold everything.
Not a legal or certification claim; a set of possibilities, not a guarantee.

**The five tenets** (the-sovereign-website): you own it, you control it, you change it (with your own AI, within a behaviour policy), you can run it anywhere, you keep it.

**Why "agentic".** 2000 gave you software to run yourself; 2026 gives you a team of agents that interviews you, builds, shows five designs, then your own AI changes the site when you ask, inside a behaviour policy (RiskMandate's policies are the alignment point).

**Open source play.** SWS is a pattern before a product: a repository holding the site as plain files, its content and schema, the agents' instructions (CLAUDE.md), the behaviour policy, checks and a deploy that run on any host. Anyone can use it without paying us, as with WordPress. A site you own must not depend on us existing. Proposed repositories in github.com/SovereignWebsite: see section 6.

**Hosted offer.** The factory sets up the repository and site, agents build it, it is served on `<name>.sovereignwebsite.site` until the owner points their own domain. Brand on sovereignwebsite.ai, .com points to it, .site for hosted sites (as github.io is kept apart from github.com).

**Prices (only what is published).** £500 anchor; £250 standing launch price; launch offer £50 or €50 (proposal in the-sovereign-website, kept at Dinis's word). One-off, no subscription. Honest notes that go beside it: the owner needs a Claude plan (or a partner) to edit; at £50 partners earn on care, not the build.

**Against whom.** Direct competitor to Wix, Squarespace, WordPress hosts and AI builders (Fliplet). Their terms cannot offer the move; that is the one claim only we can make.

**Market wedge.** Small businesses and individuals in Europe; privacy-first (no cookies unless added), multilingual (agenticcells.ai already in ten languages), European launch adding Italian, Dutch, Polish, prices in euros.

**Channels.** Partner programme (freelancers, consultancies, firms; published terms) carries across; free evaluation sites; real examples named with consent; registrar advice (your-own-registrar: OVHcloud, Hostinger, INWX) so the name is owned too.

**Relationship to AgentiCCells.** AgentiCCells stays the company selling agentic teams; SWS is its widest-market cell with its own brand. Email cell is another cell.

**What is not true yet (say so).** No SWS repository, no hosted service under the new name, no sale (agenticcells.ai states "nothing has been sold yet"). Mock-ups are proposals.
