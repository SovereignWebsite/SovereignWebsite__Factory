# Founder and personal sites: standing guidance

From Dinis Cruz, 20 November 2026, after a meeting with Kate Curtis-Evans, given in kate-curtis-evans.b's session as standing guidance for every new founder or personal site, for the sites, the conductor and the design team. The full text, kept by that site's agent: https://github.com/SovereignWebsite/Site__Founder__Kate-Curtis-Evans/blob/dev/docs/guidelines-for-founder-sites.md

Headlines:
- Five designs, always visible, with a choose-a-design control on every page.
- The milestone of the first design phase is the handover to the owner.
- Open questions go to the owner as a questions page on the site.
- Owners need not sign commits.
- Founder sites tell the specialist-becomes-founder story, with a newsroom design.

Who carries it: sites.b briefs every person-site agent with it and puts it in each request for founder, artist, designer and cv sites; design.b folds it into the design guidelines (design.agents.sovereignweb.site) and rates against it.
